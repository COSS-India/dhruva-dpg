# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2025/12/17

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy describes how the Dhruva platform (“Dhruva”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) processes data when users interact with its backend services, APIs, and inference infrastructure.

Dhruva is an infrastructure platform. It processes user-provided data only to operate the platform and does not curate, publish, rank, or moderate user content.

---

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to:
- Dhruva backend services
- Dhruva APIs and inference pipelines

Applications or services built on top of Dhruva are operated by downstream integrators, who are responsible for their own privacy practices, legal compliance, and content moderation.

---

## 3. Data We Process

### 3.1 Account and Authentication Data
Dhruva processes limited personal data strictly for authentication and operational purposes, which may include:
- Usernames
- Hashed passwords
- Session identifiers
- API keys
- Optional user-provided feedback

Passwords are not stored in plaintext.

---

### 3.2 User Inputs During Inference
Users may submit text or audio inputs for processing through Dhruva’s inference services.

- Text and audio inputs are processed in memory.
- Audio data is processed transiently and is not stored by default.

Dhruva does not require long-term storage of inference inputs for normal operation.

---

### 3.3 Operational and Technical Data
Dhruva processes technical and operational data necessary to maintain the platform, including:
- Authentication metadata
- System and usage metadata
- Error and performance logs

This data is used solely for platform operation, reliability, monitoring, and security.

---

## 4. Purpose of Data Processing
Data is processed only for the following purposes:
- User authentication and access control
- Operating backend services and APIs
- Providing inference functionality
- Ensuring system security and stability
- Troubleshooting and performance monitoring

Dhruva does not use personal data for advertising, profiling, or behavioral tracking.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Retention
- User and system metadata may be stored in a relational database.
- Session-related or ephemeral data may be cached in an in-memory data store.
- Inference inputs are not retained by default.

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill operational, security, or legal requirements and may be deleted or sanitized when no longer required. Specific retention periods may vary depending on the data type and deployment configuration.

---

## 6. Data Security
Dhruva implements reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures may include:
- Password hashing using modern cryptographic algorithms
- Token-based authentication with expiration
- Role-based access control
- Secure handling of API keys
- Encryption of data in transit where configured
- Logical isolation of operational data and inference pipelines

No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

---

## 7. Logging and Monitoring
Dhruva maintains logs and metrics for operational and security purposes, such as error detection, system health monitoring, and performance analysis.

- User inputs or inference outputs are not logged by default.
- Logging of such data may occur only when explicitly enabled and is subject to configuration, retention, and sanitization controls.

---

## 8. Data Sharing and Third Parties
Dhruva does not sell or rent personal data.

Data may be processed by third-party service providers strictly as required to operate the platform, such as infrastructure, hosting, or monitoring providers. The use of proprietary components is optional, and open alternatives may be used.

---

## 9. Content Responsibility
Dhruva is an infrastructure platform and does not curate, publish, or moderate user content.

Responsibility for content moderation, legality of content, and downstream data usage lies with integrators and application developers who build on top of the platform.

---

## 10. User Rights
Subject to applicable laws, users may have the right to:
- Request access to their personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of personal data

Requests may be subject to identity verification, legal obligations, and operational limitations.

---

## 11. Changes to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the platform or applicable requirements. Updates will be indicated by revising the “Last updated” date.
